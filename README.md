# Armored Underground

Night-city street racing in armored mechs. An original Unity 6 game that fuses
underground street racing (rain-soaked neon streets, rival crews, race events,
credits and rep, tuning) with mech assembly and boost-skating combat (parts,
energy management, quick boosts, lock-on missiles, stagger, a pulse blade).

All names, art and sound are original and generated by code in this repo:
the district, textures, materials and neon signs are built procedurally, the
mech is modelled by a Blender script, and every sound is synthesized at runtime.

## Open and play (Unity)

1. Install **Unity 6000.3.11f1** (Unity 6) through Unity Hub.
2. In Unity Hub: **Add project from disk** and pick the `ArmoredUnderground` folder.
3. On first open, click **Generate** when asked (builds the Kessel Ward district,
   textures and materials, about 1–2 minutes). You can rerun it any time from
   the menu **AU > Build Everything**.
4. Open `Assets/AU/Scenes/District.unity` (opens automatically) and press **Play**.

To make a standalone build: **AU > Build Windows Player** (writes `ArmoredUnderground/Build/`).

## Controls

| Keyboard / mouse | Gamepad | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| WASD | Left stick | Move (camera relative) |
| Mouse | Right stick | Look / aim |
| Shift (hold) | RB | Boost (drains EN) |
| Q / Left Ctrl | B | Quick boost |
| Space | A | Jump, hold to hover |
| Left mouse | RT | Arm weapon (auto-aims at lock target) |
| Right mouse | LT | Shoulder unit (missiles need a lock; mines drop behind) |
| F | X | Pulse blade lunge |
| X / Left Alt / middle mouse | LB | Overdrive (nitrous-style burst) |
| R | D-pad down | Reload |
| E | Y | Interact (Rig Shop, race markers) |
| Esc | Start | Pause |

Overdrive fills from style: skimming walls at speed, drifting, air time, slams and takedowns.

## What's in it

- **Kessel Ward**: a dense night district with wet asphalt and live street
  reflections, rain and splashes, steam vents, lit facades, storefronts, 3D neon
  tube signs, billboards, streetlights with light shafts, parked cars, traffic
  signals and string lights. Post: bloom, ACES, split toning, grain, motion blur,
  plus speed and damage effects.
- **Mech**: rigged model with procedural animation (walk, skate crouch, lean,
  air tuck, aim), thruster flames and lights. Movement: ground speed, boost,
  quick boost, hover, overdrive, wall-scrape and ram damage, stagger.
- **Combat**: rifle / gatling / scattergun / rail, homing missiles, mines,
  pulse blade, explosions, sparks, smoke, screen shake.
- **Races**: NEON MILE (2-lap circuit) and DOWNTOWN DASH (sprint, unlocks at 2 REP)
  against armed rival AI, with street gates, standings, respawns and payouts.
- **Rig Shop**: showroom with turntable; buy and equip parts across 8 slots,
  compare stats, repaint primary / accent / neon.
- **HUD**: speedometer, overdrive gauge, AP/EN, weapon status, lock-on with
  target AP and stagger, standings, lap, timer, heading-up minimap.

## Automated playtest

The player accepts `-autotest <dir>`: it drives the title, roam, garage and a full
race on autopilot and saves screenshots to `<dir>`. `-capture <dir>` takes a scenic
screenshot tour of the district.
