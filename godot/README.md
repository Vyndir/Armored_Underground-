# Iron Ascendant — Waterfront Training Hangar (Godot 4.7)

The first playable area: the training hangar on the bay, where a new pilot takes
KESTREL out of its service bay and learns to move, look, boost, quick boost,
jump and hover, wade the shallows and report in at the workshop terminal.

## Run

Open this folder in Godot 4.7 and press Play, or from a terminal:

    Godot_v4.7.2-stable_win64.exe --path godot

Controls (keyboard and mouse / gamepad, glyphs switch automatically):
move WASD / left stick · look mouse / right stick · boost Shift / RB (hold) ·
jump and hover Space / A (hold) · quick boost Q / B · interact E / Y ·
recenter camera C / R3 · controls F1 / Back · pause Esc / Start.

Options after `--`: `--skip-intro`, `--door=WxH` (hangar door comparison, default 24x11),
`--night`, `--laptop` (lighter rendering preset), `--perf <dir>` (frame timing CSV),
`--autotest <dir>` (play the course and run the QA checks). In game, F4 opens the movement
tuning panel (v02 baseline vs the previous controller).

The waterfront blockout layout, scale measurements and door comparison are documented in
`docs/layout_local.svg`, `docs/layout_region.svg` and `docs/scale_notes.md`.

The level opens with a flyover and KESTREL's release from the service bay; hold
Space / A or press E / Y to skip it, or launch with `-- --skip-intro`.

## How it is built

Everything in the level is generated by scripts when the scene starts, so the
layout can be changed in code and stays consistent:

- `scripts/site.gd` — the layout: coastline, apron, slipway, hangar, spawn.
- `scripts/build_site.gd` — terrain, bay water, apron, quay, slipway, buoy line, lamps.
- `scripts/build_hangar.gd` — hangar shell, roof plant, interior frame, bridge crane,
  service gantries, workshop, crew, yard (cabins, pickup, forklift, crates).
- `scripts/build_nature.gd` — boulders, pine forest, mountains, bridge, skyline, islets, boats.
- `scripts/art.gd`, `scripts/tex.gd` — painted banner, signage, screens and apron markings.
- `scripts/mech.gd`, `scripts/mech_rig.gd`, `scripts/chase_camera.gd` — the playable frame.
- `scripts/hud.gd` — league HUD (objective, compass, minimap, status card, prompts).
- `scripts/build_props.gd` — door portal, containers, light towers, flags, windsock,
  quay crane, workboat, clutter, signage, hall banners and welding station.
- `scripts/life.gd` — gulls, boats with wakes, walking crew, forklift loop, crane,
  windsock, lighthouse beam, steam, dust motes and welding sparks.
- `scripts/fx.gd` — footstep dust, landing shockwaves, quick-boost bursts, exhaust,
  splashes and ripples.
- `scripts/sfx.gd` — every sound and ambience loop, synthesised at startup.
- `scripts/intro.gd` — opening flyover, radio, bay release and HUD boot.
- `scripts/hangar_world.gd` — environment, lighting, onboarding steps, autotest.
- `shaders/` — water, terrain, concrete, mech armour, sky, flags and ripples.

The mech is exported from the Blender build with
`blender --background art/mech/out/v3/stray_v3.blend --python art/mech/v3/export_glb.py -- godot/assets/mech/kestrel.glb`.

## Automated check

    Godot_v4.7.2-stable_win64_console.exe --path godot -- --autotest <folder>

plays the intro, flies preset cameras, plays the whole onboarding course and saves screenshots.
